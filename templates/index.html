Clinical Workflow

How Your Recovery Journey Unfolds

From hospital admission to thriving at home — here is how our clinical supervision works.

1

Clinical Assessment

Your physician records operation details, baseline range of motion, and diagnosis in the hospital system.

2

Prescribed Plan

Your dedicated physiotherapist creates a personalized exercise program with target sets, reps, and degree milestones.

3

Camera-Guided Training

Train at home using your computer or mobile camera. Live AI monitors joint angles and provides friendly posture tips.

4

Supervised Milestones

Your clinicians review progress graphs, adjust routines as pain diminishes, and celebrate your full mobility recovery.

🏥

Hospital Safety & Clinical Decision Support

PhysioFlex provides assistive analytics and clinician supervision, not autonomous diagnosis. Always follow your physician's counsel.
